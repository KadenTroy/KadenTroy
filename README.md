Hi there, I’m Kaden 👋
I’m a neuroscience graduate from the University of Toronto Mississauga and an aspiring designer of biology platforms. I want to make biological research easier to explore and help people evaluate scientific claims for themselves.
🔭 What I’m building
NeuroSeq Discovery is a project that scans NCBI’s Gene Expression Omnibus (GEO) for brain single-cell and single-nucleus RNA sequencing studies. I’m building it to make it easier to find datasets, see what each study measured, and understand what questions its data could help answer.
🌱 What I’m learning
- Single-cell analysis with Python, Scanpy, and scVI, from quality control and clustering to exploring gene expression patterns.
- Research automation with GitHub Actions and SQLite to track newly released datasets and organize study metadata.
- Science education through interactive explanations that help readers connect biological concepts to real data.
💬 Ask me about
Neuroscience, gene expression, or how we can make research data more approachable for students who are seeing it for the first time.
